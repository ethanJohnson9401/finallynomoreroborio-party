
# Privacy and Content Policy

## About this project

Finally No More roboRIO is an unofficial FRC parody
website featuring anonymous incident submissions.

Reports are moderated before they may appear publicly.

## Information we collect

When incident submissions are enabled, the system stores:

- The submitted incident story
- A randomly generated incident identifier
- A submission key used to prevent duplicate reports
- Submission and moderation timestamps
- Moderation status

Moderator actions are recorded in a private audit log,
which includes the authenticated moderator's email address.

The application does not request a submitter's name
or email address.

However, information entered into a story may identify
individuals. Contributors should avoid including
personal or identifying information.

## Security and abuse prevention

Submissions are protected by Cloudflare Turnstile and
rate limiting.

Cloudflare may process request information, including
network identifiers, to provide hosting, security,
and abuse-prevention services.

## Publication

New reports remain private until a moderator approves
them.

Approved stories become publicly visible.

Rejected and pending reports are not published.

Moderators may reject reports containing personal
information, harassment, threats, or other inappropriate
material.

## Data retention

Retention periods are being finalized before public
submissions open.

No automated deletion schedule is currently promised.


## Removal requests

To request removal of a published incident, email
privacy@finallynomoreroborio.party.

Include the incident receipt ID, if available, or
enough information to identify the report.

Do not include passwords, credentials, or additional
sensitive personal information.

Project maintainers review removal requests and may
withdraw an approved incident from public publication.

Withdrawal removes an incident from public display
but does not automatically erase it from the database,
moderation records, or existing backups.


## Backups

Database backups may contain incident reports and
moderation information.

Backups are treated as private operational data and
must not be published in the project repository.

## Changes

This policy may be updated as the submission and
moderation systems evolve.

The current version is maintained in the project's
public GitHub repository.
